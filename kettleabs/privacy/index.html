KettleAbs Privacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026. Publisher: Cruz App Labs LLC.

KettleAbs 1.0 is a walking timer, local activity journal, private photo journal and general educational app. No KettleAbs account is required.

Information on your device

The app stores preferences, walking timer recovery and completed activity history locally. It does not measure your location, route, steps, distance or calories. The timer pauses when you leave the screen or app.

Photos you choose

You may select individual images through the system picker. KettleAbs creates a resized JPEG copy in its private app storage and removes the original embedded metadata by re-rendering the image. App photo copies are excluded from operating-system backups. The app does not upload photos or offer cloud sync. Your original images and any backups or cloud services holding those originals remain unchanged.

No developer data collection

KettleAbs does not send your activity history or photos to Cruz App Labs LLC. This version includes no advertising SDK, analytics service, tracking, in-app purchases or subscription. The app does not sell or share your personal information for advertising.

Your choices and deletion

Delete individual photos and sessions in Progress. Privacy & app includes a control to clear all activity history. Removing the app removes its app-private data, subject to your platform's backup and restore behavior. Activity preferences may be included in operating-system backups; private app photo copies are excluded. No KettleAbs cloud recovery is provided.

External links and support

Educational source links open your browser. Those sites and your browser process information under their own policies. If you email support, we receive the information you choose to send and use it to respond. Do not send health information or progress photos to support. Apple and Google may process store, installation, diagnostics or device information under their own policies and your device settings.

Security and general education

Photos are hidden when the app becomes inactive. App storage protections cannot protect against every device, backup or screenshot risk; keep your device secured. KettleAbs provides general education, not medical advice, and makes no promise of a particular appearance.

Changes and contact

Material changes will be reflected in an updated policy. Contact Cruz App Labs LLC at support@cruzapplabs.com.
