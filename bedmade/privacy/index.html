BedMade

Privacy Policy

Effective July 23, 2026 · Last updated July 23, 2026

Privacy at a glance: BedMade processes bedroom photos, Screen Time selections, and routine settings on the device. Cruz App Labs does not operate a server that receives bedroom photos, Parent PINs, Screen Time selections, or child activity from BedMade.

1. Who we are

BedMade is provided by Cruz App Labs LLC (“Cruz App Labs,” “we,” “us,” or “our”). This policy explains how BedMade handles information when a parent or child uses the BedMade iPhone app.

Questions may be sent to support@cruzapplabs.com.

2. BedMade’s approach to privacy

BedMade is designed to work without a Cruz App Labs account, advertising profile, analytics service, or cloud photo library. The app uses Apple frameworks and local device storage to provide its core features.

Cruz App Labs does not add advertising, cross-app tracking, third-party analytics, or social-media tracking SDKs to BedMade.

3. Information handled by the app

Information How it is used Where it is handled
Bed reference photo Used as the parent-approved reference for future bed checks. Stored in protected local app storage and requested to be excluded from device backups.
Daily bed-check photo Compared with the saved reference to help decide whether the bed appears made. Processed on the device in memory and discarded after the check, including when the check closes or the app backgrounds.
Apple Screen Time selections Used to protect only the apps and categories selected by the parent and to enforce the chosen play plan. Handled locally through Apple Family Controls, Managed Settings, and Device Activity frameworks.
Routine and protection settings Includes the weekday and weekend play plan, daily limits, current approval state, Parent Pause, and temporary overrides. Stored locally in BedMade’s protected app-group storage so the app and its Apple extensions can use the same settings.
Parent PIN verifier Used to confirm parent-only actions. The PIN digits are not stored. BedMade stores a salted, iterated verifier in the device Keychain.
Purchase entitlement status Used to determine whether Annual or Lifetime BedMade Plus access is active. Provided through Apple StoreKit. Cruz App Labs does not receive payment-card details.

4. Camera access and photos

BedMade requests camera permission so a parent can create a bed reference and a child can complete a daily bed check. Bedroom photos are not uploaded to Cruz App Labs.

  • The parent-approved reference remains on the device until it is replaced, deleted through BedMade, removed through Start Over, or removed with the app’s local data.
  • A daily check photo is temporary. It is not added to the Photos library by BedMade and is discarded after the comparison or when the check is abandoned.
  • BedMade does not place bedroom image bytes into analytics or application logs.

5. Apple Screen Time information

BedMade uses Apple’s parental-control frameworks to let a parent choose apps or categories to protect and to apply the parent’s schedule and daily limit. BedMade does not create an independent inventory of all apps installed on the device and does not use website-domain selections for protection.

Screen Time authorization can be changed or revoked through Apple’s settings. If authorization is denied or revoked, BedMade may be unable to protect selected apps until access is restored.

6. Purchases

In-app purchases and subscriptions are offered through Apple. Apple processes the transaction and may handle information under Apple’s own privacy policy. BedMade receives StoreKit transaction and entitlement information needed to activate, restore, renew, expire, or revoke access. Cruz App Labs does not receive your full payment-card number.

7. Information we do not collect through BedMade

BedMade does not send Cruz App Labs:

  • Bedroom photos or image comparison data
  • The Parent PIN or its Keychain verifier
  • Selected app or category tokens
  • Child app-usage history
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Precise location
  • Contacts, messages, microphone recordings, or health data

8. Data sharing

Cruz App Labs does not sell BedMade user data and does not share BedMade information with advertisers or data brokers. BedMade uses Apple system services required for camera access, Screen Time controls, app distribution, and in-app purchases.

We may disclose information only when legally required and only to the extent we actually possess it. Because BedMade’s core personal information remains on the device, Cruz App Labs generally does not possess that information.

9. Data retention and deletion

BedMade’s settings and reference photo remain on the device while needed for the app to function. The app provides parent-controlled options to replace or delete the reference photo and to start over.

Use BedMade’s Start Over feature before deleting the app when you want BedMade to remove its saved setup, reference photo, and Parent PIN material through the app’s own deletion process.

10. Children’s privacy

BedMade is designed for use within a family under a parent’s control. Cruz App Labs does not knowingly collect personal information from children through a BedMade server or account system because BedMade does not provide such a server or account system. Parents control setup, protected-app selection, schedules, purchases, and parent-only decisions.

11. Security

BedMade uses Apple platform protections, including protected local file storage, the device Keychain, App Groups, and on-device processing. No security method is perfect, but BedMade is designed to minimize transmission and keep sensitive family information on the device.

12. Website privacy

Cruz App Labs does not add advertising, analytics, or tracking cookies to this privacy-policy page. The hosting and network providers used to deliver the webpage may process routine technical information such as IP addresses and request logs under their own policies.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when BedMade’s features, legal obligations, or privacy practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are published.
