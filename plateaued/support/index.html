Support

Help with Plateaued for iPhone.

Contact Cruz App Labs

Email us with the app version, your iPhone and iOS version, and a short description of what happened. Do not include passwords or sensitive medical information.

Email support@cruzapplabs.com

Walk steps are unavailable

Open iPhone Settings and review Plateaued’s Motion & Fitness access. You can still time and record a walk when a step count is unavailable. Plateaued does not need your location to count steps during an in-app walk.

Notifications are unavailable

Open iPhone Settings, choose Notifications, then Plateaued. Notification permission is optional, and the app remains usable without reminders.

Purchases and subscriptions

Use Restore Purchases in Plateaued if a prior purchase is not recognized. Apple handles billing and subscription changes. You can manage your subscription with Apple.

Your information

Use You > Privacy & data controls to export a summary or erase locally stored app information. Read the Plateaued Privacy Policy for details.

Basic troubleshooting

  • Confirm that Plateaued is updated to the latest App Store version.
  • Close and reopen the app.
  • Restart the iPhone if a system permission or sensor appears stuck.
  • If the issue continues, email support before erasing app data.

Medical or urgent concerns

Plateaued provides general lifestyle coaching, not medical care. Stop exercising and seek appropriate medical help for severe or concerning symptoms. Contact local emergency services for an emergency.
