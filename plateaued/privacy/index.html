Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026

Your information Plateaued does not require an account, run ads, use third-party analytics, or sell your personal information. Your coaching information is stored locally on your device.

Information you choose to provide

Plateaued can store information you enter or create, including your optional first name, profile preferences, wake and bedtime schedule, meal-timing entries, activity records, steps recorded during Plateaued walks, weight entries, check-ins, notification preferences, plan history, and coaching history. Version 1.1 also stores the safety and care answers you choose to provide, including adult eligibility, pregnancy or breastfeeding, eating-disorder history, clinician-directed restrictions, and appetite or intake limitations. These answers guide the availability of adaptive coaching.

Motion information

If you grant permission, Plateaued uses Apple’s motion services to show a live step count during a walk you start in the app. Motion permission is optional. This feature uses step-count information from the device’s motion services.

You can change motion access in iPhone Settings. If access is unavailable, Plateaued explains that the step value is unavailable and the walk timer can still operate.

Optional Apple Health access

Version 1.1 offers optional read access to body weight and step counts in Apple Health. You choose the data types to allow. Plateaued uses readable data on your device to show weight history and activity information and support habit coaching. Manual weight entries take priority over Health imports on the same local day. All-day step totals are kept distinct from the walks you record in Plateaued.

You can connect, sync, or stop automatic imports in You > Activity sources, and manage Health permissions in Apple Health or device Settings. Disconnecting stops future imports. Earlier imports are retained as local copies until you erase Plateaued’s saved data. Manage original Health records in Apple Health and local copies in Plateaued’s data controls. Apple may limit the history available to read.

How information is used

Your information is used to operate the app, present your plan and progress, preserve your history, and generate future coaching plans on your device. Plateaued does not use health or activity information for advertising, profiling by data brokers, or cross-app tracking.

Storage, export, and deletion

Plateaued’s plan and user-entered information are stored in the app’s local storage on your device. Cruz App Labs does not operate an account or cloud service that receives this information.

Version 1.1 marks its state, backup and recovery files for exclusion from device backups. Apple controls the final backup and restore behavior. Older versions may include local app information in device backups depending on your Apple settings.

Version 1.1 provides a complete, versioned JSON export of saved app data from You > Privacy & data controls. Earlier versions provide a summary export. You can also erase local app data there. The export can include health imports, safety answers, weight entries, logs, setup drafts and plan history, so choose its destination carefully. Copies you export are controlled by the destination you select. Deleting the app removes its local records; keep an export if you want your own copy.

Purchases and notifications

Apple processes App Store purchases and subscription management. Plateaued receives transaction and entitlement status from Apple, not your payment-card details. If you enable notifications, iOS delivers locally scheduled reminders; notification permission can be changed in iPhone Settings.

Websites, email, and outside services

Opening a website, subscription-management page, or email link leaves Plateaued and is handled under the selected service’s policies. This website’s hosting provider may process routine request information, such as IP address and browser details, to deliver and secure the site. Cruz App Labs does not add advertising trackers or analytics cookies to this policy page.

Children and medical information

Plateaued is intended for adults. It provides general lifestyle coaching and is not a medical device, diagnosis, treatment, or emergency service. Do not use Plateaued as a substitute for professional medical advice or emergency care.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Material changes will be communicated in the app when appropriate.
