Privacy Policy

Effective September 4, 2026

The short version: Plateaued does not require an account, run ads, use third-party analytics, or sell your personal information. Your coaching information is stored locally on your device.

Information you choose to provide

Plateaued can store information you enter or create, including your optional first name, profile preferences, wake and bedtime schedule, meal-timing entries, activity records, steps recorded during Plateaued walks, weight entries, check-ins, notification preferences, plan history, and coaching history.

Motion information

If you grant permission, Plateaued uses Apple’s motion services to show a live step count during a walk you start in the app. Motion permission is optional. Plateaued does not use your location for this feature and does not currently read from or write to Apple Health.

You can change motion access in iPhone Settings. If access is unavailable, Plateaued explains that the step value is unavailable and the walk timer can still operate.

How information is used

Your information is used to operate the app, present your plan and progress, preserve your history, and generate future coaching plans on your device. Plateaued does not use health or activity information for advertising, profiling by data brokers, or cross-app tracking.

Storage, export, and deletion

Plateaued’s plan and user-entered information are stored in the app’s local storage on your device. Cruz App Labs does not operate an account or cloud service that receives this information.

You can export a summary or erase local app data from You > Privacy & data controls. Deleting the app also removes its locally stored information unless you exported it elsewhere first. Copies you share or export are controlled by the destination you select.

Purchases and notifications

Apple processes App Store purchases and subscription management. Plateaued receives transaction and entitlement status from Apple, not your payment-card details. If you enable notifications, iOS delivers locally scheduled reminders; notification permission can be changed in iPhone Settings.

Websites, email, and outside services

Opening a website, subscription-management page, or email link leaves Plateaued and is handled under the selected service’s policies. This website’s hosting provider may process routine request information, such as IP address and browser details, to deliver and secure the site. Cruz App Labs does not add advertising trackers or analytics cookies to this policy page.

Children and medical information

Plateaued is intended for adults. It provides general lifestyle coaching and is not a medical device, diagnosis, treatment, or emergency service. Do not use Plateaued as a substitute for professional medical advice or emergency care.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Material changes will be communicated in the app when appropriate.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email support@cruzapplabs.com.
