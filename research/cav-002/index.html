CAV-002 · SEPARATE VOLUNTARY RESEARCH

Could a simpler note
make planning clearer?

Cruz App Labs LLC is preparing a small study comparing fictional note-planning examples with free alternatives. We want to learn whether the idea is useful before building an app.

Adults 18+Up to 12 volunteersAbout 15 minutes

What taking part involves

  1. Read the privacy notice and choose whether to consent.
  2. Compare three concepts, a free note, an existing tool, and “none.”
  3. Organize supplied fictional statements in two note formats.
  4. Optionally share fixed-choice preferences, including a hypothetical price question.

No personal stories, medical history, health records, medication dose, weight, symptoms, or diagnosis. No free-text answers, uploads, recordings, medical advice, paid recruitment, incentives, or payments. You do not need to disclose medication use or any health condition.

This study is separate from our products. We do not recruit from other products’ users, mailing lists, research, or the founder’s personal contacts.

Participation

Recruitment is not open yet. We are completing and verifying the privacy and deletion safeguards before accepting volunteers.
